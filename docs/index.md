## 問天地安好

Contains links and documents from `#share-resources` in the CEAL discord server, as well as more general guides, tutorials, and helpful resources about classical East Asian languages.

**Resources are included on this site because members found them helpful and posted them in the discord. The CEAL moderation team and website team does not officially endorse nor verify the veracity of any external links posted here. If you download anything, always verify the safety of the resource.**

This site is still in progress, some sections may be incomplete.
