# Dictionaries

## Kangxi Dictionary

#### Kangxi Dictionary in MDD and MDX forms.
Accurate to the forms used in the original edition. Based on comparison against the high-resolution Kangxi 55 / 1716 武英殿刻本 scans from the Harvard-Yenching Library.  Headwords reorganised for searchability. It also arranges by pronunciation and meaning, and also notes 古文 and 異體字.
