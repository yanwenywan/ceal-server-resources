#### 啓蒙篇 / 啟蒙篇 계몽편/gyemongpyeon/qǐméngpiān

`unharmeddrudge` Chapters for Enlightening the Ignorant (unknown author)

Joseon-era Classical Chinese primer teaching basic vocabulary, grammar and essential cultural concepts through easy-to-follow sentence structures. Highly recommended for beginners, especially those learning as a second language.

[Link (google drive)](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1ptkhn70a9PPkaMKzCi8Fh-XbFaNEiRee/view?usp=sharing)

#### 兒學篇 이학편 / ihakpyeon / érxuépián

`unharmeddrudge` Child Learning Book 丁若鏞 정약용 Jeong Yakyong

An excellent textbook for essential Classical Chinese vocabulary of everyday concepts and objects, arranged by related categories. 

[Link (google drive)](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1Ao3t-7x6CsnqlWdxmccZJqeCcMiSVzDL)
